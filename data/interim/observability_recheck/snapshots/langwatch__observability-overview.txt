# Observability & Tracing

Monitor, debug, and optimize your LLM applications with comprehensive observability and tracing capabilities

> Page: https://langwatch.ai/docs/observability/overview
> Source: https://github.com/langwatch/langwatch/blob/main/docs/observability/overview.mdx
> Index of all pages: https://langwatch.ai/docs/llms.txt

LangWatch tracks every interaction inside your LLM applications, helps you debug issues, and shows you how your AI systems work in production.

![LangWatch Observability Dashboard](https://langwatch.ai/docs/images/llm-observability/overview-1680.062220236a49.webp)

## Core Features

- [Real-time Tracing](https://langwatch.ai/docs/concepts): Watch every LLM call and tool usage as it happens, with full context.
- [User Events](https://langwatch.ai/docs/user-events/overview): See how users actually interact with your AI - thumbs up, selections, custom events.
- [Cost Tracking](https://langwatch.ai/docs/integration/python/tutorials/tracking-llm-costs): Know exactly how much each model call costs you, down to the token.
- **Monitor Performance**: Spot slow calls and bottlenecks before your users complain.
- [Automations & Alerts](https://langwatch.ai/docs/features/automations): Get notified when things go wrong, or when costs spike unexpectedly.
- [Embedded Analytics](https://langwatch.ai/docs/features/embedded-analytics): Drop dashboards right into your app so your team can see what's happening.

## How it works

Add a few lines to your code and LangWatch starts tracking everything:

1. **Add the SDK** - Drop in a few lines of code to your existing app
2. **We track everything** - Automatically captures all your LLM calls and interactions
3. **See it live** - Watch what's happening in real-time through the dashboard
4. **Debug** - Click into any trace to see exactly what went wrong
